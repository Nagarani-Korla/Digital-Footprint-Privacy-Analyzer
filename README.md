# TraceGuard AI

## AI Digital Footprint & Privacy Risk Analyzer

### 📌 Project Overview

TraceGuard AI is a web-based privacy analysis system that analyzes text and identifies personal information that may create privacy risks.

The system detects information such as email addresses, phone numbers, names, organizations, locations, and dates. It calculates a privacy risk score, identifies compound privacy risks, explains possible inferences, and provides privacy recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Detect personal information from user-provided text.
- Calculate a privacy risk score from 0–100.
- Classify privacy risk as Low, Medium, or High.
- Detect compound privacy risks.
- Identify possible information that someone could infer.
- Provide privacy recommendations.
- Sanitize sensitive information from text.

---

## 🚀 Features

- Personal information detection
- Email detection
- Phone number detection
- Person/name detection
- Organization detection
- Location detection
- Date detection
- Privacy risk scoring
- Low / Medium / High risk classification
- Risk breakdown
- Compound privacy risk detection
- Privacy inference analysis
- Privacy recommendations
- Text sanitization
- Risk progress bar
